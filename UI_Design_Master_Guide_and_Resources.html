6. Technical Deep-Dive: How Frame Scrubbing Works

The jewelery/ project achieves buttery-smooth 60fps frame scrubbing using a lightweight architecture rather than heavy video decoding:

  • Image Sequence: 481 individual WebP frames (e.g., c001.webp to c481.webp) are stored in public/frames/. WebP files provide ~80% size savings over PNGs while retaining crisp details.
  • HTML5 Canvas: Instead of switching <img> DOM tags (which causes flickering), frames are preloaded into memory and drawn directly to an HTML5 <canvas> context.
  • Scroll Interpolation: A scroll hook calculates the user's progress (0.0 to 1.0) and maps it to the exact frame index: Math.floor(progress * totalFrames).
  • Lenis Smooth Scroll: Lenis normalizes wheel deltas across macOS, Windows, and mobile, eliminating stutter during fast scroll scrubbing.
